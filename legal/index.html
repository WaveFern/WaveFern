The fine print for WaveFern: what is fictional, who made the game, and the third-party software and fonts it uses.

WaveFern is fiction

WaveFern is a fictional music career game. Its city, characters, record labels, songs, charts, streams, money and events are made up for entertainment. Any resemblance to real people or events, other than the parodies described below, is a coincidence.

In-game brands are parodies

The apps, shops and awards in the game are invented parodies, made for fun, including Spotifly, Instagrime, Amazoom, the Gphone, Threadz, FestivalApp and the Gramophone awards. WaveFern is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by Spotify, Instagram or Meta, Amazon, Apple, the Recording Academy, or any other company. All real trademarks belong to their owners and any references are for parody and commentary only.

Parody artists

Some characters in the game are parodies of famous music artists, with spoof names such as Taylor Swiff or Drakeo. They are made-up characters for comedy and commentary. Their songs, albums, messages, prices, looks, stats and behaviour are invented, and nothing they "say" in the game was said by the real person. The real artists are not affiliated with WaveFern and have not endorsed it.

If you are an artist or represent one and would like a parody changed or removed from the game, send us a message through the contact form and we will deal with it quickly.

No real money

There are no purchases, loot boxes, gambling or ads in WaveFern. In-game money and rewards cannot be bought, sold or cashed out.
