Stadium and club shows

From the WaveFern Wiki

In each tour city the stadium is on the plaza and the club downtown. Walk in, plan the show in the dressing room, then play it.[1]

Contents
  1. Before the show
  2. Production
  3. Show score

Before the show

  • Setlist: starts from your World Tour plan for that stop, or your top songs. The last song is the encore.
  • Rehearse up to 2 times a day: you need 20 energy and it uses 8.
  • A show needs 25 energy and must start between 12:00 and 02:00.

Show score

score = 0.12 + 0.5 × minigames + 0.18 × crowd hype + 0.1 × song quality + 0.05 × rehearsals (max 2) + production

Between songs you talk to the crowd. Greeting them in the local language works best (+0.16 hype). A stadium show has an intermission and an encore and runs 6-10 in-game hours.
