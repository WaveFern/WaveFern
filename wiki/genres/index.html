There are 9 genres. Each has its own beat styles, its own daily hype, and belongs to a family of similar sounds.[1]

Hype (trending)

Every morning each genre's hype is rerolled between ×0.8 and ×1.3. It multiplies the daily streams of every song in that genre, old ones included. On the Make a song screen a genre above ×1.12 shows HOT and one below ×0.92 shows COLD.

Genre focus

Fans reward a steady sound. When you release a song, the five songs you released before it decide its focus, a multiplier it keeps on its daily streams for good.

share = (1 per same genre + 0.8 per same family) ÷ 5 previous releases run = how many of your latest releases in a row are in this family (max 5) focus = 0.75 + 0.5 × share + 0.03 × run

The families are:

  • Hip-Hop, Rap, R&B and Latin: Hip-Hop, Rap, R&B, Latin
  • Pop and Country: Pop, Country
  • Rock: Rock
  • Lo-fi and Electronic: Lo-fi, Electronic
NoteYour very first release has nothing to compare against, so its focus is ×1. A toast tells you when a release gets +5% or more, or −5% or less.
TipPick one family and stay in it for most of your releases. You can still switch: the penalty fades as your last five releases fill up with the new family.

Tips

  • Pick a family you like and stick with it for a while. Hopping between sounds costs you.
  • A HOT genre is a nice bonus for one day. It is not worth breaking a long run in your family for.
  • If you want to switch sound, do it gradually through a genre in the same family.
