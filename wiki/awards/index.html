Gramophone Awards

From the WaveFern Wiki
Gramophone Awards
NeedsFame Level 225
Cooldown15 days
CategorySong of the Year
PrizeGramophone Trophy

The awards hall stands at the end of your street. From Fame Level 225 you can check in (once every 15 days), submit one song, and watch the ceremony.[1]

How to win

You're up against four of 16 big stars, and only the single best song wins. The judges look mostly at how good your song is, with a small bonus for how many streams it has.

Tips

  • Submit your best song, not your newest one.
  • Quality matters far more than streams here. A great song with few plays beats an average song with millions.
  • The stars you face are very good. If you lose, keep improving your gear and come back in 15 days.
  • Mentors, a home and pets all add quality, so they help here too.

A win puts a Gramophone Trophy in your Storage: furniture that isn't sold anywhere.
