Spotifly

From the WaveFern Wiki

Spotifly is where your music lives. Open it on the laptop (or the Spotifly app on your phone).[1]

  • Setup: pick your artist name. You need one before recording.
  • Upload: title drafts and release them, one by one or all at once.
  • Stats: streams, earnings and listeners by day, and per-song history.
  • Artist page: your profile, top 5 songs by popularity (or all songs), albums.
  • Browse: other artists' pages.
  • Promote songs (Promotions) and build Albums.
