Tour cities

From the WaveFern Wiki

Every destination becomes a city you can walk around when you land: signs in the local language, its own colours and traffic, an airport, a hotel, shops, a club and a stadium.[1]

  • Landmark selfie: once a day per city, for 30 + √listeners × 0.25 followers and an Instagrime post.
  • Hotel: check in at reception and sleep in your room. You wake up there.
  • Shops: every shopfront opens the room its sign names, in any language: restaurants (pizza, tacos, sushi, noodles, burgers and grills serve their own dishes), cafés and tea houses, bakeries, markets, ice cream and juice bars, convenience stores, bars, souvenir shops (postcards, snow globes, T-shirts, fridge magnets), record shops, bookshops, boutiques and sneaker shops (real clothes for your wardrobe), pharmacies, florists, barbers (the haircut menu), laundromats, electronics shops, beauty shops, massage parlours, bike shops (ride across town), cinemas, arcades and karaoke bars. Each shopfront has a window display of what it sells, and each counter opens a menu in the shop's own style (a florist's card, a bakery's paper menu, a café chalkboard, neon for bars, arcades and record shops, and so on).
  • Once a day per city: hand a bouquet to a fan (15 + √listeners × 0.15 followers), sing at karaoke (20 + √listeners × 0.2), play your song on an electronics shop's demo speakers (8 + √listeners × 0.1) or set an arcade high score (10 + √listeners × 0.1).
