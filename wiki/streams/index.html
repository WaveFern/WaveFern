Every released song earns streams every day. Streams build up through the day (twice as fast at night) and are paid the next morning at $0.05 per stream, minus any label cut.[1]

Daily streams

daily = quality × 0.36 × random(0.75-1.25) × genre hype (×0.8-1.3, rerolled daily) × age decay (0.86^days + 0.015) × fame 1.5^fame tier × (1 + min(1, √listeners ÷ 200)) × interest 0.3 + 0.7 × interest × reach 1 + promo×0.3 + decks×0.2 + content kit + home×0.1 × quality clamp((quality ÷ 50)^0.8, 0.45, 2.5) × collab (feature)² × (1 + 0.5 × min(1.5, quality ÷ 100)) × focus genre focus (see Genres) × 1.5 on release day

On top of that, a collab brings the featured artist's fans: about 1% of their monthly listeners a day, decaying the same way.

Fresh Finds

While you have under 3,000 monthly listeners, Spotifly's Fresh Finds playlist for new artists plays your songs too. Each song gets extra daily streams on top of the formula above, and the morning results say how many came from the playlist:

fresh finds = 28 × (1 − listeners ÷ 3,000) × age decay × clamp(quality ÷ 10, 0.6, 2.2) × random(0.8-1.2) × 1.5 on release day (still under the cap)

So an average first song gets about 30 extra streams on release day. The playlist fades out as your own fans grow, and is gone by 3,000 monthly listeners.

Other boosts that stack on top: Promotions, album tracks, the catalogue halo, your Gphone model and Radio and TV slots.

Interest

Interest is a 0-100% measure of how much people are talking about you. Each morning it drops by 10%, then gains 6% for every song you released yesterday. Albums, radio and diss tracks also move it. Releasing steadily keeps it high.

Whether a song is a hit, goes viral or is normal is rolled once, on its release day.

Hits

hit chance = 0.08 × clamp(quality ÷ 60, 0.5, 1.6) a hit streams ×3-6

Going viral

viral chance = 0.0003 × min(1, quality ÷ 90)^3 a viral song gets 5M + listeners × 20-50 extra streams over 14 days

The cap

So one song can't run away forever, daily streams are capped:

cap = quality² × 25 × 1.5^fame tier × hit multiplier

Payouts

Each morning you're paid yesterday's streams × $0.05. If you're signed, the label keeps its cut first. Earnings show on the morning summary and in Spotifly › Stats.

Tips

  • Release often. Interest fades every day you do not put something out.
  • A song does most of its streaming in its first few weeks, so a steady flow beats one big drop.
  • Quality lifts everything else in the formula, so it is the best place to spend effort.
