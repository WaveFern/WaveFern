Promotions

From the WaveFern Wiki

From Instagrime you can pay to promote a released song. The price grows with your monthly listeners. A promoted song streams its multiplier times its normal amount every day of the campaign, and you gain followers when it starts.[1]

PromotionDaysStreamsPriceFollowers
Boost3×1.5$40 + $0.002 × listeners3-12 + 1% of listeners
Campaign5×2$150 + $0.006 × listeners3-12 + 2% of listeners
Blitz7×3$600 + $0.015 × listeners3-12 + 4% of listeners
TipA promotion multiplies the song's normal daily streams, so it is worth most on a strong, fresh song in a HOT genre.
