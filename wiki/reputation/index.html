Reputation

From the WaveFern Wiki

Reputation is a hidden score that starts at 0.[1]

What changes it

EventChange
A rival answers your diss track−2 to +3
Cancelling a festival booking less than 2 days before−2
Missing your festival slot−3
Selfie with an artist on a Gphone 12 or newer+1

What it does

  • Collab fees: 1% cheaper per point (up to 10%).
  • DMs: 3% more likely per point, and bigger artists reach out more.
  • Bigger Record labels need a minimum reputation.
