From the main menu pick Multiplayer: create a room, or join one with a friend's code. A room is a band of up to 4 players who live in the starter house together.[1]

You pick your instrument the first time you join, and keep it for that band career. Everyone spawns in the studio, which has every member's instrument.

How it works

  • Each member has their own band career for that room, saved on their own device. It never touches your solo careers or cloud save.
  • The host's clock is the band's clock. Only the host's bed ends the day.
  • Recording and festival sets need every online member present and ready.
  • Band songs: whoever starts the take names it, everyone records together, and releasing it releases it for the whole band. Its quality is the members' average plus 3 per extra member.
  • If one member runs out of goes, the take is scrapped for everyone.
  • If the host leaves, the room closes and the others carry on alone.

Emotes

Press B for the emote wheel: 👋 Wave · 🕺 Dance · 👏 Clap · 🙌 Cheer · 😂 Laugh · 🎸 Air guitar · 🌀 Spin · ❤️ Love.

Connection

Players connect browser to browser. When a direct link can't be made (school networks, strict mobile data), the game switches to a relay after 9 seconds, so everyone still shares one room.
