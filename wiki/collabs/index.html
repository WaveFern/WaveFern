A collab is a song with another artist. You either pay them (you messaged them) or they pay you (they messaged you). Their fans come along: a featured song gets about 1% of their monthly listeners in extra streams each day.[1]

Who messages you

Each day 0 DMs (20%), 1 (40%) or 2 (40%) can arrive, never more than 3 waiting. On your first day 2-3 small artists say hello. Who writes depends on size:

  • Artists about your size are the most likely.
  • A bit smaller: common. Much smaller: rare, unless you worked together before or share a genre.
  • Bigger: falls off fast with the gap, helped by your growth this week, your interest and your reputation.
  • Your recent genres: ×1.6 for the same genre, ×1.25 for a related one, ×0.85 otherwise.
  • Artists with 50k+ listeners only DM you once you have 25k. Panic Man Dan waits until you have 5,000.

The price

what they charge = their fee × size factor × markup × reach × repeat × reputation size factor = 0.18 + 0.82 × A ÷ (A + 1500) (A = their listeners) markup = 0.92 to 1.12, fixed per artist reach = ((A + 5) ÷ (you + 5))^0.2, kept within 0.75 to 1.35 repeat = ×0.92 if you've worked together before reputation = −1% per point (up to −10%) what they pay you = 55% of your own market price, never more than 80% of their fee

Haggling

Type your offer in Messages in plain words, like "how about $120?", "two fifty" or "1.5k". Artists counter, and bigger names usually move less on price. A lone "100" means "keep it 100", not an offer.

The meeting

Once you agree, press Confirm & pay (or Accept & get paid). The artist waits at the meeting place (gold beacon). Walk up to them to chat for 1-3 exchanges: pick a tone (hype, chill, pro, joke, cocky) that suits their personality (chill, hype, shy, formal, goofy). Their opinion goes from −3 to +3 and changes the song: ±4 quality and ±12% feature strength per point.

feature strength = (1.05 + min(0.3, fee ÷ 6000)) × (1 + opinion × 0.12)

Tips

  • Pay attention to how an artist talks before you pick a tone in the meeting. A tone that suits them goes a long way.
  • If an artist barely moves on price, do not waste rounds lowballing them.
  • Artists who message you pay you. Saying yes to a few early on is easy money and new fans.
  • The feature is worth more when the song itself is good, so do not spend big on a collab you rush.
