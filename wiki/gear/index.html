Studio gear

From the WaveFern Wiki

Gear is bought on the laptop (Shop). Each item has 3 upgrades. Prices grow 6× per level, and the effect grows with it. Some upgrades only unlock at a fame level.[1]

Tips

  • Cheap first levels across many items often beat one expensive top level.
  • Getting heard gear does not make songs better, but it makes good songs earn more.
  • Some upgrades are locked behind fame. Plan for them before you have the money.
