Days, time and energy

From the WaveFern Wiki

Real time12 minutes

Song cost15 energy (1 less per chair level)

A full in-game day lasts 12 real minutes, from 06:00 to 06:00 the next morning. About nine songs fit in one day.[1]

The clock

The first half of the day runs from 06:00 to 20:00; the second half is night, from 20:00 to 06:00. The clock keeps running while the computer or phone is open.

Energy

  • You wake with 100 energy after sleeping in your bed.
  • Recording a song costs 15 energy (1 less per level of chair; 7.5 for your part in a collab). A festival set costs 15 and a tour show 25.
  • Energy slowly comes back by itself during the day, about 36 over a full day.
  • Food tops it up: the corner shop, City Cafe, Goober Eats and festival stalls.

Sleeping

You can only go to bed at night (after 20:00), or earlier if your energy is under 15. Sleeping ends the day: yesterday's streams are paid, your monthly listeners are updated, every genre's hype is rerolled and new messages arrive.

Passing outIf you are still awake at 06:00 you pass out where you stand and wake with only 70 energy.

What happens overnight

  • Streams for the day are completed and paid at the stream rate (after any label cut).
  • Interest decays by 10% and gains 6% for each song you released that day.
  • Monthly listeners and followers update (followers grow by 5% of the day's streams).
  • Genre hype rerolls between ×0.8 and ×1.3.
  • A new mentor may unlock, artists may DM you, labels may make offers, and new quests roll.
