Controls

From the WaveFern Wiki

WaveFern plays with a keyboard and mouse, or with touch controls on phones and tablets.[1]

KeyWhat it does
WASD / arrowsWalk
ShiftSprint (uses stamina)
E / Space / EnterInteract: doors, desks, shops, people, mic
FStorage and furniture build mode at home
RRotate furniture while placing it
NWorld map (and the taxi)
MMute music
GOpen your phone
BEmote wheel in a band room
EscPause menu: settings, save, quit
123Pick a rhyme in the Lyrics minigame
Space (hold)Raise the level in the Mix minigame

Walking and stamina

You walk at 3.3 m/s and sprint at 6.5 m/s. Sprinting drains stamina by 15 per second; it refills at 10 per second when you stop. If you run it to zero you are winded and can't sprint again until it is back to 30.

Touch

On a phone or tablet a joystick appears bottom left, with buttons for sprint and interact. Menus, the phone and the computer work by tapping.
