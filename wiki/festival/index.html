Park Fields Festival

From the WaveFern Wiki
Park Fields Festival
WhenEvery 7th day
WhereThe field east of Park Avenue
BookFestivalApp, 2+ days ahead
SetUp to 4 hours
Energy15 per set
Pay per song$0.08 × stage × crowd

A festival opens on every day that is a multiple of 7 (day 7, 14, 21...). The day before, a crew builds it; the day after, they take it down.[1]

Crowd and pay

crowd = min(max, base × 1.055^(your level − stage level)) × (0.9 + min(0.2, average quality ÷ 400)) × 1.15 for a collab song pay after each song = $0.08 × stage multiplier × crowd streams = crowd × 0.1 × (0.5 + minigame score) followers = crowd × 0.015 × (0.5 + minigame score)

Tips

  • Book the biggest stage your fame allows. The crowd and pay grow fast with stage size.
  • Put your best songs in the setlist. The crowd notices quality.
  • Arrive at the stage a little early so you do not miss your slot.
