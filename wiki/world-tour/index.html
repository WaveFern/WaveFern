World Tour

From the WaveFern Wiki
World Tour
UnlocksFame Level 275
Price$250,000 once
StopsUp to 5
Songs per stopUp to 12
Venue cooldown14 days
ShowsOne a day

The World Tour app appears in the App Barn at Fame Level 275. It costs $250,000 once. Plan on its world map: click a continent, then a country, then add its stops, up to 5. Each stop gets a setlist (songs or a whole album). Then book Flights and the airport and fly there.[1]

How a show pays

fill = clamp(0.55 + 0.012 × levels above the venue's + 0.25 × preparation, 0.3, 1) attendance = capacity × fill × (0.7 + 0.3 × show score) money = attendance × (ticket price × 0.05 + 1) followers = attendance × 0.12 × (0.5 + score) streams = attendance × 18 × (0.5 + score) (to the songs you played)

Tips

  • Rehearse before big shows. It costs a little energy and makes a real difference.
  • Learn how to greet each crowd. Talking to them well lifts the whole show.
  • Production costs a lot at first, so save it for the stadiums.
