Mentors

From the WaveFern Wiki

There are 5 mentors, each inspired by a music legend. One unlocks each time you reach the next monthly listener milestone, and every mentor adds +2 quality to every song (and 8 experience). Meet them on the laptop under Legends.[1]

MentorMonthly listenersTheir advice
Mentor 110Perfectionism and showmanship: rehearse until it feels effortless.
Mentor 240Own your sound: learn every instrument in the room.
Mentor 3120Write honest songs. A plain story lands harder than a fancy one.
Mentor 4400Obsess over the mix. Details make the record.
Mentor 51000Experiment fearlessly with tone and texture.
