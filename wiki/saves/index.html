Saves and careers

From the WaveFern Wiki

The main menu has two career slots. Each career saves on its own, so nothing leaks between them.[1]

How saving works

  • The game autosaves to your browser as you play.
  • Sign in with Google from the main menu to keep your career in the cloud and play it on any device.
  • The pause menu can export a save code (starts with FW3-). Paste it back in to load it anywhere. Older FW2- and FW1- codes still load.
  • If a save ever fails to load it is kept as a recoverable backup instead of being lost. Backups show under Recovered backups on the career screen: press Recover, or drag one onto a slot.
Band careersBand rooms keep a separate band career for each room, on your device. It never touches your two career slots or your cloud save.
