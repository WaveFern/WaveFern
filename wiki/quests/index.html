Quests

From the WaveFern Wiki

The Quests app (preinstalled on the laptop) gives you 3 daily quests and 3 weekly quests. Finish the whole set to get paid. Quests are rolled once and saved, so reloading never changes them.[1]

daily reward = $30 × 1.04^fame level (rounded to $5) weekly reward = 10 × the daily reward

Daily quests

  • Record 2-4 songs (more at fame 10 and 30)
  • Release 1-3 songs
  • Release a song at or above your recent average quality + 3
  • Post on Instagrime
  • Send 4 messages
  • Walk or run 400-700 m

Weekly quests

  • Get 35% more streams than last week
  • Earn 35% more from music than last week
  • Gain 35% more followers than last week
  • Release 5-9 songs
  • Record 12-18 songs
  • Release 3 songs at your recent average quality + 6
  • Release an album
  • Post on Instagrime on 5 different days
  • Walk or run 3,000-6,000 m

Weeks run day 1-7, 8-14 and so on. The night after a day still counts for that day, so overnight streams land in the right week. Each set never has two quests of the same kind.
