Messages and Big Vee

From the WaveFern Wiki

Messages holds every chat: artists who DM you (see Collabs, fees and haggling), label offers and Big Vee, your manager. Pin chats to keep them on top, clear them, or press New for suggested people.[1]

Big Vee

Big Vee is pinned by default. Text a question in plain words and Big Vee answers from your current save. Topics: controls, making a track, uploading and money, Spotifly, billboards, height, collabs, Messages, gear, clothes, sleep and energy, the map and cars, festivals, fame and saving.
