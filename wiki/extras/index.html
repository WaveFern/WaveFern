Extras: sub-genres, diss tracks, 808s and hi hats

From the WaveFern Wiki

Open Extras on the Make a song screen to choose a sub-genre, a diss track, your 808 and your hi hats.[1]

Sub-genre

Every genre has its own beat styles, like Boom Bap for Hip-Hop or UK Drill for Rap. Surprise me (the default) lets each song pick one at random. Or pick a sub-genre from the list, grouped by genre: its genre button on the Make a song screen shows the sub-genre name, and recording that genre uses it. Recording any other genre still gets a surprise. Your pick is remembered for the next song and saved with each song, so it keeps that beat. It changes how the song sounds, not its quality or streams.

Hip-Hop: Boom Bap, Jazz Rap, G-Funk, Soul Chop, Southern Bounce, Breakbeat
Rap: Trap, UK Drill, NY Drill, Rage, Plugg, Phonk
Pop: Dance Pop, Synth-pop, Pop Ballad, Tropical, Bedroom Pop, Disco Pop

Latin: Reggaeton, Latin Trap, Bachata, Salsa, Cumbia, Latin Pop

808 and hi hats

Both pickers offer Auto (the genre's own pick), Built-in (the classic synthesised sound) or one of the named samples. Your choice is remembered for the next song and saved with each song, so it plays the same every time. They change how the song sounds, not its quality.

Diss tracks

Tick Diss track and pick a rival to make a song called No Love For [rival]. Releasing it is a gamble:

  • On release, interest changes by somewhere between −8% and +25%.
  • 2 to 5 days later the rival answers. That moves interest by −12% to +20% and your reputation by −2 to +3.

See Rivals for the list of rivals.
