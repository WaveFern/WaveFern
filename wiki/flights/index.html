Flights and the airport

From the WaveFern Wiki
Swiftwing Airways
FlightsOne a day per route
Departures07:00 to 23:30
BookUp to 3 days ahead
Refund70% if cancelled 2h+ ahead
Gates1 to 6

Swiftwing is the game's airline. Book from the Swiftwing app (computer or Gphone) or at the ticket desk in any airport. Fernfield International is at the far west end of Fern Road.[1]

Fares and flight times

flight time = max(2, round(km ÷ 800 + 0.7)) hours fare = round((180 + km × 0.11) ÷ 10) × 10, varying a little day to day

The rules

StepWhen
BookUntil 60 minutes before departure, up to 3 days ahead
Check in24 hours to 45 minutes before (app or a check-in desk)
SecurityNeeds a boarding pass
BoardingOpens 2 hours before at the gate
Gate closes10 minutes before departure: the ticket is lost
CancelMore than 2 hours before, for a 70% refund

The flight

At your gate press E at the door, take your marked seat and the plane leaves. The clock moves on by the flight time. A flight that runs past 06:00 counts as your night's sleep.

TipStranded abroad with no money? Swiftwing gives you a free seat home.
