Taxi

From the map you can call a taxi to any place. You get one trip a day: one ride out, then one ride back to where it picked you up. After that it's gone until tomorrow.

Weather

Each day rolls its weather: Sunny, Rain, Fog, Thunderstorm. About 90% of days are sunny, 5% rain, 3% fog and 2% storms. Day 1 is always sunny.
