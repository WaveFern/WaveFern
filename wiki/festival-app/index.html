FestivalApp and bookings

From the WaveFern Wiki

FestivalApp is free and installed for you. Pick a festival, a stage your fame allows and a free slot.[1]

SlotTime
Slot 110:00 to 14:00
Slot 214:00 to 18:00
Slot 318:00 to 22:00
Slot 422:00 to 02:00
  • Book at least 2 days before the festival. Booking is free.
  • Other acts have already taken some slots; at least one per stage is always left free.
  • Build a setlist from released songs and whole albums. Each song lasts 30-55 in-game minutes, so 4-8 songs fill a slot. The setlist can't go over 4 hours.
  • Editing or cancelling is free while the festival is 2+ days away. Cancelling later costs 2 reputation.
  • On the day, be at your stage's steps during your slot. Miss it and the slot is given away, and you lose 3 reputation.
