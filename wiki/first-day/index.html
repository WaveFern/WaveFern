You start as an unknown artist with $100, a starter house with a tiny studio and a laptop. The goal: record songs, release them on Spotifly, and grow from nobody to a global star.[1]

Gameplay

  1. Pick an artist name on the laptop (E at the desk, then Spotifly). You cannot record until you have one.
  2. Walk to the mic in your studio and press E. Pick a genre and play the three recording minigames. Each song costs 15 energy.
  3. Back at the laptop, open Spotifly › Upload, give the draft a title and release it. Streams start right away.
  4. Sleep in your bed to end the day. Overnight, yesterday's Streams are paid out and your monthly listeners update.
  5. Spend the money on Studio gear so your next songs are better, and repeat.

The tutorial

Big Vee, your manager, walks you through the basics: walk, pick your artist name, record a song and release it. A glowing line on the ground leads you to each place, and on the laptop his box shows over the screen and the button to click is outlined. After the basics he only drops in now and then, when you find something new (your first DM, the street, a car, a shop, the airport...), with at least 45 seconds between tips.

Tips offPress Tips off on his box, or use the pause menu, and he stays quiet (so do the small helper tips). Turn them back on from the pause menu. You get the $150 starting gift either way.

Your first release is added to Spotifly's Fresh Finds playlist, so your first songs get real plays from day one.

Early goals

  • Record a few songs in one genre family: the genre focus bonus rewards a steady sound.
  • Buy a better microphone first: it gives the most quality per level.
  • Install Instagrime and post once a day for followers.
  • Reach 10 monthly listeners to unlock your first mentor.

Tips

  • Your first songs will not be great. That is normal: keep making them.
  • Sleep before 06:00, or you will start the next day tired.
