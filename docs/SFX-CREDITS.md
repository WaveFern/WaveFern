# Sound effect credits

All game sound effects (clicks, pops, pings, chimes, cash and coins, purchases, level ups, beat judgements, errors, notices, whooshes, eating) are
**synthesised live in the browser with the Web Audio API** by `09-settings-effects.js` and `200-sfx-polish.js`.
They are original to WaveFern, use no recorded or third-party audio files, and need no attribution or licence.
Each effect is layered from a noise transient, a tuned inharmonic body (bell, glass or marimba partials), a sub thump and a reverb tail, with small random pitch, timing and pan changes so repeats never sound identical.
