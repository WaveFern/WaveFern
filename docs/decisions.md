# Decisions

- 2026-11-18: `S.money` is guarded at the property level (accessor in `247-money-guard.js`) instead of hunting every `S.money+=x`. Two places zero a non-finite cash value (29-v26 new-day code, `scCheck` in 12-save-seal), so any NaN write during the day became "$0 after sleep". Refusing non-finite writes fixes every source at once and logs the culprit's stack in `fw_mlog`.
- 2026-11-18: `$$` shows cents below $100 (only when there are cents); bigger amounts keep the short K/M/B form.
- 2026-11-18: Battery: the main loop caps to 30 fps in mode `'pc'` (still desk scene behind the monitor) and 10 fps while the full-screen Mastery panel is open (it has its own renderer). dt is clamped to 0.05 s, so caps must stay at 20 fps or more wherever game time runs.
- 2026-11-19: Security boundary is the Firebase rules only; client checks (`isAdmin`, `fwIsDev`, `ad` flag in band rooms, the `/dev/` password) are UI. Band-room ids and band-song ids are reduced to `[A-Za-z0-9_-]` and never `__proto__`/`constructor`/`prototype` at the point they arrive (`cln`, `idOk`, `idc` in `161-multiplayer.js`). Rules now require a Google sign-in everywhere (anonymous accounts are free to mint). `fw-bundle` CSP left loose on purpose (inline handlers); see `docs/SECURITY_CHECKLIST.md`.
- 2026-11-23: Releases: fixes ship at once as patch numbers; features merge dark (`FW_DEV` gate or `HELD` flag) and go live together on a Friday drop (next major, v56.0.0) with a `news-data.js` card. Version lines are edited only by `tools/bump.js`, which can re-apply a branch's entry after a merge (`renumber`). No reset, rename, history rewrite or long-lived hold branch: the earlier numbering pain was every PR editing the same version lines, not the scheme.
