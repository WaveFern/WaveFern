Privacy Policy

Last updated 30 October 2026

WaveFern is a free browser game made by James Hutchinson and Rowan Burton ("we", "us"). This page explains what information the game and this website use, why, and the choices you have. We keep it short because we collect very little.

The short version. You can play without an account, and then your game is saved only in your own browser. Signing in with Google is optional and is used for cloud saves and band rooms. Google Analytics only runs if you allow it, and you can turn it off any time in Settings > Game. We never sell your data and there are no ads.

What we collect and why

Playing as a guest

Your careers, settings and backups are saved in your browser's local storage on your device. They are not sent to us. Clearing your browser's site data deletes them.

Google sign-in and cloud saves (optional)

If you choose Sign in with Google, sign-in is handled by Google through Firebase Authentication. We receive your Google account's name, email address, profile picture and a user ID. Your name and picture are shown on the title screen.

Your cloud save is stored in Google Cloud Firestore and contains your saved game, your in-game day, your artist name and the time it was saved. The game's admin (one of the makers) can view saves to help with problems and to send in-game gifts. Other players cannot see your save.

Band rooms (multiplayer)

Band rooms need a Google sign-in. To run a room we use the room code, the band name, the player name you type, your instrument and the game updates that go between band members (such as songs, chats with in-game artists and positions). These are sent through Firebase Realtime Database (hosted by Google in Europe) and through PeerJS, which helps browsers connect to each other directly. When browsers connect directly, the other players' browsers can see your IP address. A small room record (room code, band name, player count and the host's user ID) is used to show your rooms list. Only play in rooms with people you know.

Analytics (only if you allow it)

When you first open the game we ask whether you allow anonymous usage stats. If you say yes, Google Analytics for Firebase counts visits and a few game events: starting a career, recording or releasing a song, releasing an album, signing to a label and reaching day milestones, along with the game version and in-game day. We do not send names, emails, save data or account IDs. Google will still receive standard technical information such as your IP address (used for an approximate location), device and browser type, and will store an identifier in a cookie or local storage. You can change your choice any time in Settings > Game > Analytics. If you say no, Google Analytics is not loaded at all.

Contact form

If you use the contact form, we receive your name, your email address if you give it, and your message. The form is delivered to our inbox by Web3Forms. We only use it to read and reply to your message.

Hosting, fonts and code libraries

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Fonts come from Google Fonts, and code libraries are loaded from cdnjs (Cloudflare), jsDelivr and unpkg. Like any website, these services receive your IP address and browser details when your browser downloads files from them, and they may keep short-term logs for security and performance. We do not get access to those logs.

Legal bases (UK and EU)

If you are in the UK or the EU, we rely on: providing the game you asked for for saves, sign-in and band rooms; your consent for analytics (which you can withdraw any time); and our legitimate interests for keeping the game secure and replying to messages.

Who we share it with

We do not sell or rent personal information and there is no advertising in WaveFern. Information is handled by the services that run the game for us: Google (Firebase Authentication, Firestore, Realtime Database and Analytics), PeerJS, Web3Forms, GitHub and the font and code providers above. Some of them process data outside your country, including in the United States, under their own safeguards. We may also share information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Saves on your device: until you delete the career or clear your browser data.
  • Cloud saves and sign-in records: until you ask us to delete them.
  • Band room data: only as long as needed to run the room and your rooms list.
  • Analytics: kept by Google for the limited period set in our Analytics settings, then deleted.
  • Contact messages: as long as needed to deal with them.

Your choices and rights

You can play without signing in, turn analytics off in Settings, sign out at any time, and delete careers in the game. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to ask for a copy of your data, to correct it, to delete it, or to object to how it is used. To ask, or to have your cloud save and sign-in record deleted, send us a message through the contact form and say which Google account it is about. If you are in the UK or EU you can also complain to your data protection authority (in the UK, the ICO).

Children

WaveFern is a family-friendly game, but it is not aimed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Google sign-in, band rooms and the contact form are for players aged 13 or over (or older where your country sets a higher age, which is up to 16 in some EU countries). Younger players can play as a guest, with saves kept only on their device, and should leave analytics switched off unless a parent agrees. If you are a parent and think your child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change what the game collects, we will update this page and the date at the top. Big changes will also be noted in the version history.
